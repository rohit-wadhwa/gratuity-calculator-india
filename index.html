How gratuity is calculated

  • Formula for covered employers: (Basic + DA × 15 × completed years) ÷ 26. Uncovered employers use ÷ 30.
  • Only completed years count. A final partial year over 6 months rounds up; 6 months or less is dropped.
  • The amount uses your last drawn Basic + DA — applied across your whole tenure, not your historical salary each year.
  • Up to ₹20 lakh is exempt under Section 10(10); anything above is taxable as salary income.
  • The 5-year minimum is waived on death or permanent disability. Some employers honour 4 years + 240 days as the 5th year.
After how many years is gratuity payable?
Gratuity becomes payable after completing 5 years of continuous service. This minimum is waived in case of death or permanent disability.
How much gratuity is tax-free?
Up to ₹20 lakh is exempt from income tax under Section 10(10). Any amount beyond ₹20 lakh is taxable as salary income at your slab rate.
Which salary components are used?
Only Basic salary plus Dearness Allowance (DA). HRA, special allowance, bonus and other components are not included.
What about fixed-term employees?
Under the Code on Social Security, 2020 (effective Nov 2025), fixed-term employees can become eligible after just 1 year of service. The 5-year rule still applies to regular employees.
